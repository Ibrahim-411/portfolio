About

Search, AI answers, paid media and WordPress. One person.

I work across SEO, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), paid media and WordPress development. On search, I do keyword research, technical audits, on-page and local SEO.

On paid, I run Amazon Sponsored Products and Meta Ads, and I focus less on vanity ROAS and more on whether the campaign actually clears its break-even.
